BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

A few little
things to know.

Is the villa completely private?+

Yes. Tranquil Terrace has two separate private villas: Araliya and Olu. Each has one king bedroom, one private bathroom and its own plunge pool.

How many guests can stay?+

Villa Araliya and Villa Olu each accommodate up to two guests.

How do I reserve my stay?+

Select your dates and number of guests, then enquire on WhatsApp or check the villa on Booking.com, Agoda or Airbnb. The booking platform or villa team confirms availability and reservation terms.

Can I arrange something special?+

Tell us what you have in mind when you enquire. Breakfast, dining and celebration arrangements are subject to availability and may have additional charges.

Where can I check rates and arrival times?+

Google Maps lists check-in from 1:00 PM and check-out by 10:00 AM. Contact us with your preferred dates for current rates, payment details and cancellation terms before confirming your booking.
