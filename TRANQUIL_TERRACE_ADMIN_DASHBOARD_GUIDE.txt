TAB 1: RESERVATIONS MANAGEMENT (වෙන්කිරීම් කළමනාකරණය)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[English]
This is your main daily operational hub for managing all guest bookings.
Key Capabilities:
1. Search & Filter:
   - Filter by Villa: View Araliya, Olu, or All.
   - Filter by Status: Inquiry, Pending, Confirmed, Checked In, Checked Out, 
     Cancelled.
   - Filter by Payment: Unpaid, Partial, Paid, Refunded.
   - Instant search by Guest Name, Phone, Email, or Reference ID.
2. Add New Booking (+ Add Reservation):
   - Manually enter walk-in, phone, or direct WhatsApp bookings.
   - Choose stay packages with built-in official timings:
     * Day Package: 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM
     * Night Package: 5:00 PM – 9:00 AM (Next Day)
     * Day & Night Package: 10:00 AM – 9:00 AM (Next Day) / Option B
   - Set price in LKR or USD, enter advance payments, and calculate balance due.
3. Overbooking Prevention:
   - The cloud database strictly prevents double-booking the same villa on 
     the same date. Overlapping stays are blocked automatically.
4. 1-Click WhatsApp Concierge:
   - Click the green WhatsApp button next to any guest to open WhatsApp Web or 
     mobile app with a personalized message (Booking confirmation, directions, 
     payment receipt, check-in instructions).
5. Print / Export Vouchers:
   - Generate official, beautifully formatted booking confirmation vouchers 
     and invoices to print or save as PDF for guests.

TAB 3: CALENDAR SYNC & OTA INTEGRATION (Airbnb / Booking.com Sync)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[English]
Two-way automated iCal synchronization with global travel portals.
Key Capabilities:
- Live export feeds for Villa Araliya and Villa Olu (`.ics` URLs).
- Connect to Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda, Vrbo, and Google Calendar.
